Privacy Policy

Olive Press Tycoon by Shlewett Games · Last updated 28 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

Your game progress

Your progress and sound setting are saved only on your own device (browser local storage). They never leave your device and are removed if you clear the app's data or tap "Start over".

Third-party services

The game is served from GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and basic request details to deliver the files. See the GitHub and Google privacy policies.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
